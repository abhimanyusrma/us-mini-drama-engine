# THE US SHORT-FORM DRAMA PLAYBOOK V1.0

## 1. SCRIPTING & PACING MANIFESTO (US MARKET)
- **The 1.5-Second Hook:** Shot 1 must start in media res with extreme emotional conflict, a visual shock, or an aggressive confrontation. No establishing shots of buildings or slow character introductions.
- **Micro-Dialogue Rule:** Maximum 5 words per dialogue line. Use contemporary, natural American English. 
- **Structural Beats (45-Second Target):**
  * 0-2s: The Hook (The betrayal or shock is exposed).
  * 3-15s: Escalation / Arguing back and forth.
  * 16-30s: The Micro-Twist or complication.
  * 31-40s: The Climax / Confrontation peak.
  * 41-45s: Loopable Cliffhanger (End on a question or unresolved reaction shot).

## 2. VIDEOEXPRESS ENGINE ROUTING
- **Visual Style:** Photorealistic (Cinematic), Creative Mode OFF.
- **US Environmental Anchors:** Every image prompt generated must lock the setting to recognizable American backdrops (e.g., "in a sleek Manhattan office", "a sunlit California suburban kitchen", "a dark Chicago restaurant booth").
- **Asset Specs:** 4-second base clips, High/FullHD 1080×1920 portrait format. Turn public gallery OFF.

## 3. TIMELINE & EDITING AUTOMATION
- **Pacing:** Keep individual shots on the timeline to a strict maximum of 2.5 to 3 seconds. Slower visual pacing kills US viewer retention.
- **Aggressive Dead-Air Trim:** Instruct the timeline editor to cut and delete at a speed of 50 px/s. Aggressively slice out the initial and trailing frozen frames where characters are just opening/closing mouths without speaking. The dialogue must hit rapid-fire.
- **Export:** Export the final MP4 and provide the user with the direct download link from the "My Videos" dashboard.
